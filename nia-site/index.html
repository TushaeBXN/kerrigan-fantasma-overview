Ten domains. Real tools. Verified facts.

Nia doesn't summarize Wikipedia. She has purpose-built tools for each domain — law databases, calculators, letter generators, live search. No guessing.

Credit Repair

All three bureaus + sub-bureaus (LexisNexis, ChexSystems, Early Warning, NCTUE, Innovis). FCRA § 611 disputes, Metro 2 violations, SOL by state. Generates certified-mail letters.

Debt Relief

Charge-off strategy, debt validation (FDCPA § 809), cease and desist, pay-for-delete, time-barred debt defense. Statutes of limitations for all 50 states.

Student Loans

PSLF tracking, IDR plan comparison, Borrower Defense, Closed School Discharge. Knows ITT Tech, Corinthian, DeVry, Full Sail, and more by name.

House Hacking

FHA 3.5% down, 2-4 unit real cash flow math. Tells you your exact net monthly cost after tenants pay. BRRRR strategy. First-time buyer programs.

Business Acquisition

SBA 7a analysis, SDE valuation multiples, seller financing, deal verdict. Tells you if a price is fair and what your monthly net is after debt service.

Investing & Savings

Dividend ETFs, REITs, CD laddering, I-Bonds, share certificates, infinite banking. Real compound interest math on your actual savings goal.

Business Formation

LLC vs S-Corp vs nonprofit. Entity stacking. EIN, operating agreements, annual compliance. Building business credit separate from personal.

Life Insurance

Term vs whole vs universal. Infinite banking. Overfunded life insurance as a tax-advantaged growth vehicle. Generational wealth transfer.

Rights & Resources

FCRA, FDCPA, Fair Housing, Title VII. Your rights by domain, by state. Routed to free legal aid — nonprofit credit counselors, HUD housing counselors.

Run Nia in two minutes

All you need is Ollama installed locally.

# Clone and set up
git clone https://github.com/TushaeBXN/nia-ai.git
cd nia-ai
pip install -r requirements.txt
bash setup.sh

# Start Nia
python -m nia.cli

# Or chat directly via Ollama
ollama run nia-ft

Nia runs on your machine. Nothing you share is stored or sent anywhere. Want to fine-tune her on your own data? TRAINING.md walks you through it. Pull requests welcome.
